# KAFO shop process sheet v0 — form + rivet + hinge

Product: KAFO (knee–ankle–foot orthosis) — uprights, knee joints, thigh/shank cuffs  
Status: ASSUMED dims until calipers/cast — do not cut production lots from this alone.

## Rivets (locked)
| Hole | Rivet | Use |
|------|-------|-----|
| Ø6 | 10.5 mm pipe **or** flat | Structural bar ↔ cuff — **coupon trial both** |
| Ø4 | 12.5 mm flat Al | Soft goods / secondary |

---

## A. Cuff forming (thigh + shank)

| Step | Op | Check |
|------|-----|-------|
| A1 | Cut blank from composite/HDPE sheet (thigh & shank patterns) | Blank size vs flat pattern |
| A2 | Dry heat / vacuum form to contour | No thin spots, no burns |
| A3 | Trim edges, radius sharp corners | Smooth skin contact edge |
| A4 | Drill/ream Ø6 lands for uprights (stack with bar when possible) | Hole Ø, pitch match bar |
| A5 | Drill Ø4 only for strap packs | No structural load on Ø4 |

**Gate A:** fit on mock limb / cast; rock at upright lands = rework holes or form.

---

## B. Upright bar (Al)

| Step | Op | Check |
|------|-----|-------|
| B1 | Cut rectangular bar to length | Length vs KAFO upright |
| B2 | Drill/ream Ø5.1 knee holes both ends (or one end if split upright) | Match hinge plates |
| B3 | Drill Ø6 cuff rivet pattern | Match cuff lands |
| B4 | Deburr all holes | No burr into cuff |

**Gate B:** dry-fit bar in hinge fork + cuff holes align.

---

## C. Hinge plates + snap pin (mild steel / hardened pin)

| Step | Op | Check |
|------|-----|-------|
| C1 | Blank 2 plates per joint | Thickness, fork gap = bar T |
| C2 | Stack-drill/ream Ø5.1 | Common hole, parallel faces |
| C3 | Make pin (head + shank + snap groove) | Groove for E-clip/ring |
| C4 | Assemble: plates + bar in fork → pin → retainer | Snap feel, no walk-out |
| C5 | Cycle open/close full knee ROM | No bind, play OK |
| C6 | Finish plates (Zn/paint); keep bore/pin clean | — |

**Gate C:** insert force, retention shake test, lateral play, free ROM.

---

## D. Riveting (after coupon winner for Ø6)

| Step | Op | Check |
|------|-----|-------|
| D0 | Coupon: 3× pipe + 3× flat Ø6×10.5 on scrap Al+cuff | Pick winner |
| D1 | Clamp bar to cuff; set **all Ø6** structural first | Sequence centre→outer |
| D2 | Set Ø4 Al flats for straps | Pads/Velcro secure |
| D3 | Inspect | No cuff crush/white ring; bar flush; heads seated |

**Gate D:** no rock at bar–cuff; sample peel/pull on coupon only.

---

## E. Soft goods + final

| Step | Op | Check |
|------|-----|-------|
| E1 | Rivet/sew Velcro strap packs | Length after fit |
| E2 | Bilateral: medial + lateral uprights | ML symmetry |
| E3 | Patient/cast fit trial | Pressure points, knee axis |
| E4 | Mark rev / lot on BOM | Traceability |

---

## Order of work (one brace)
1. Form & trim cuffs (A)  
2. Make bars (B) + hinges (C) as knee subassemblies  
3. Coupon Ø6 (D0) → rivet uprights to cuffs (D)  
4. Straps (E) → fit trial  

## Safety / quality
- No structural load on plastic/Ø4 rivets  
- Deburr before skin-contact fit  
- Replace ASSUMED lengths after measure before production
